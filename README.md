# WikiMasters — Bilingual Wikipedia Trading Card Game

> 🎮 Play online right now: [https://wikimasters.org](https://wikimasters.org)

**WikiMasters** is a multiplayer online trading card game (TCG) built entirely on the depth of world knowledge. Turn real-world history, science, philosophy, culture, and nature into collectible, strategic trading cards.

---

## 🌟 Core Gameplay Features

1. **Card Packs & Booster Openings**
   - Open standard, thematic, and legendary booster packs.
   - Collect cards spanning world history, physics, art, cinema, astronomy, and biology.

2. **Real-Time Quiz & Trivia Duels**
   - Duel other scholars and players worldwide in real-time knowledge arenas.
   - Strategically place cards and answer contextual questions to conquer territory.

3. **Global Market & Player Auctions**
   - Buy, sell, and trade rare cards in an open player-driven auction house.
   - Earn in-game coins, level up card attributes, and complete encyclopedic albums.

4. **Guilds & Community Hall of Fame**
   - Join forces with guilds to pool resources, conduct research expeditions, and compete in seasonal guild wars.
